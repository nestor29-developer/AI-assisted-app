# Northwind Traders Employee Handbook

This handbook describes how people at Northwind Traders work together. It applies to every full-time employee in the Lisbon headquarters and the Austin office. Where a team has its own rules, the team lead explains them during onboarding. Questions about this handbook go to the People team at people@northwind.example.

## Working hours and offices

Core hours are nine to three local time, Monday to Friday. Employees choose their own start time as long as they are reachable during core hours. The Lisbon headquarters is open from 7:30 to 19:00 and the Austin office from 8:00 to 18:00. Visitors must sign in at reception and wear a visitor badge at all times. Both offices are closed from December 25 to January 1.

Meeting rooms are booked through the calendar. A room that is not used within ten minutes of the start time is released automatically. Phone booths cannot be booked and are limited to fifteen minutes per call.

## Leave

Full-time employees accrue 1.5 vacation days per month, up to a maximum balance of 30 days. Unused vacation days expire on March 31 of the following year. A carry-over request must be approved in writing by the direct manager before the end of February.

Sick leave is paid for up to 10 days per calendar year. A doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive sick days. Employees should tell their manager before the start of the working day when they are unwell.

Bereavement leave is 5 paid days for an immediate family member and 2 paid days for other relatives. Jury duty is paid in full. Public holidays follow the local calendar of each office and do not count against vacation days.

## Remote work

Remote work requires written approval from the direct manager each quarter. New hires work on site for their first 90 days. Employees working remotely must be reachable during core hours and must join team meetings by video.

Northwind pays a one-time home office allowance of 400 dollars. It can be spent on a desk, a chair or a monitor and is claimed through the expense system with receipts. The allowance is not repeated when an employee changes teams.

Working from another country for more than two weeks in a year needs approval from the People team because of tax and insurance rules.

## Expenses

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 60 dollars per day. Receipts are required for any single expense above 25 dollars and must be submitted within 30 days. Expenses submitted later than 30 days are reimbursed only with director approval.

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 220 dollars per night in cities with more than one million inhabitants and up to 150 dollars per night elsewhere.

Mileage for approved use of a private car is reimbursed at 0.67 dollars per mile. Taxis and ride shares are reimbursed when public transport is not practical, for example after 22:00 or when carrying equipment.

Managers approve expenses up to 500 dollars. Anything above 500 dollars needs the approval of a director. Personal items, fines and alcohol are never reimbursed.

## Travel

Business trips are booked through TripDesk at least 14 days in advance. Flights shorter than six hours are booked in economy class. Flights of six hours or longer may be booked in premium economy when the trip starts or ends on a weekend.

For international trips Northwind pays a per-diem of 85 dollars instead of reimbursing individual meals. The per-diem is paid together with the next salary. Travel insurance is included and is activated automatically when the trip is booked in TripDesk.

An employee who needs to change a booking must do so at least 24 hours before departure. Changes made later are charged to the employee's team budget.

## Equipment and devices

New employees receive a laptop on their first day. Laptops are replaced every 3 years, or earlier when they can no longer run the required security software. Employees may request a second monitor from the IT desk without further approval.

A lost or stolen device must be reported to the IT desk within 2 hours so that it can be wiped remotely. Personal devices must not be used to open customer data. Software that is not on the approved list may only be installed after a security review.

## Information security

Passwords must have at least 14 characters. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every Northwind account, including email and the code repository. Passwords must never be shared, not even with the IT desk.

USB storage devices are not permitted on company laptops. On public Wi-Fi the company VPN must be switched on before any work is done. Customer data must be stored only in approved systems and never in personal cloud storage.

All employees complete the annual security training in October. Phishing simulations run every quarter, and repeated failures lead to a short refresher course. Suspected incidents must be reported to security@northwind.example immediately.

## Onboarding

Every new employee follows a 30, 60 and 90 day plan agreed with their manager during the first week. A buddy from another team answers everyday questions during the first three months. The probation period lasts 90 days and ends with a conversation about the plan.

On the first day the IT desk hands over the laptop and sets up accounts, and the People team explains payroll and benefits. The first week also includes an introduction to the product and a visit to the other office by video.

## Benefits

Health insurance starts on the first day of the month after the employee joins. Northwind matches retirement contributions up to 4 percent of salary. Every employee has a learning budget of 1,200 dollars per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused learning budget does not roll over.

A gym membership is reimbursed up to 30 dollars per month. The employee assistance program offers free and confidential counselling 24 hours a day. Employees in Lisbon receive a monthly public transport pass.

## Performance reviews

Performance reviews are held twice a year, in June and in December. Employees write a short self-assessment and the manager adds feedback from colleagues. Ratings use a scale from 1 to 5, where 3 means fully meets expectations.

Promotions are decided once a year in the January cycle. A promotion needs a written case from the manager and a calibration discussion between managers. Feedback outside the review cycle is encouraged and does not need a form.

## Code of conduct

Northwind does not tolerate harassment or discrimination of any kind. Concerns can be raised with a manager, the People team or the confidential ethics line at ethics@northwind.example. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is a disciplinary matter.

Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to the manager within 14 days of becoming aware of them. Gifts from suppliers or customers above 50 dollars must be reported to the People team and may have to be returned.

## Working with customers

Customer data is confidential. Employees may only access it when they need it for their work, and every access to the customer database is logged. Screenshots that contain customer data must not be posted in public channels.

Customer complaints are answered within one business day, and a complaint that cannot be solved by the first person is passed to the team lead the same day. Refunds above 1,000 dollars need the approval of a director.

## Health and safety

First aid kits are kept at every reception desk and on every floor. Fire drills take place twice a year, and everyone follows the signs to the assembly point in the car park. Accidents, however small, are reported to the facilities team on the same day.

Employees who work at a screen for long periods are entitled to a free eye test every two years, with a contribution of up to 80 dollars towards glasses. Height-adjustable desks are available on request and need no approval.

## Commuting and parking

The Austin office has 40 parking spaces, allocated by lottery every quarter. Both offices have secure bike storage and showers. Employees who cycle to work at least eight days in a month receive a bonus of 25 dollars in their next pay.

Employees can also claim a bike repair voucher of 60 dollars per year. Electric vehicle charging is available in the Austin car park and is free for employees.

## Learning and development

Besides the yearly budget, every team has a half day each month for learning together. Internal talks take place every other Thursday and are open to everyone. Employees who finish a certified course receive a recognition bonus of 150 dollars.

Conference attendance is approved by the manager and booked through TripDesk like any other trip. Employees who attend a conference share what they learned with their team within two weeks.

## Mentoring

Employees can ask for a mentor from another team for six months. Mentors meet their mentee every two weeks for 45 minutes. The People team matches mentors with mentees within ten working days of a request.

## Volunteering

Each employee has two paid volunteering days per year. Teams may also volunteer together on one day per quarter. Volunteering days must be agreed with the manager at least a week in advance.

## Celebrations

The company holds one summer party in July and one winter party in December. Birthdays are celebrated with a card signed by the team. The budget for the winter party is 40 dollars per person. Nobody is required to attend a party.

## Procurement

Purchases above 2,000 dollars need three written quotes. Purchases above 10,000 dollars need the approval of the finance director. Suppliers must be registered by the procurement team before the first order, and supplier invoices are paid within 45 days.

## Data privacy for employees

Employee records are kept for the length of employment plus six years. Employees can ask the People team for a copy of their own data at any time, and the copy is provided within 30 days. Photos taken at company events are published only with the consent of the people shown.

## Office etiquette

Shared kitchens are cleaned by everyone, and dishes go into the dishwasher before six in the evening. Noise-sensitive work is done in the quiet zones on the second floor. Food with a strong smell is eaten in the kitchen, not at the desks. Meetings with visitors take place in the meeting rooms, never in the open space.

## Internal tools

Tickets for the IT desk are opened through the service portal, and urgent problems can also be phoned in. The IT desk answers urgent tickets within one hour and other tickets within one business day. Software licences are requested through the same portal and are reviewed weekly.

The company wiki is the single place for team documentation. Pages that have not been edited for a year are marked for review by their owners.

## Sustainability

Both offices run on renewable electricity. Business trips shorter than 500 kilometres are made by train whenever a direct connection exists. Single-use plastic cups are not provided in the kitchens, and old laptops are refurbished and donated whenever possible.

## Public holidays

Lisbon observes the Portuguese national holidays, including April 25 and June 10. The Austin office observes the US federal holidays and, in addition, the day after Thanksgiving. When a holiday falls on a weekend no replacement day is given. An employee who has to work on a holiday receives the same amount of time off within the next three months.

## Team meetings

Every team holds a weekly planning meeting of at most 45 minutes and a monthly retrospective of at most 90 minutes. Agendas are shared one day before the meeting. All-hands meetings are held on the first Tuesday of each month and are recorded for colleagues in other time zones.

## Raising concerns anonymously

Concerns about illegal or unethical behaviour can be reported anonymously through the ethics line. Reports are acknowledged within seven days, and the person who made the report receives feedback within three months. Nobody is asked to reveal their identity in order to be heard.

## Company equipment on the road

Equipment taken on a business trip stays in hand luggage and is never left in a car. Company phones are covered by travel insurance only when they are locked with a passcode. Chargers and adapters are available from the IT desk without a ticket, and cables are not replaced when lost.

## Leaving Northwind

An employee who resigns gives one month of notice, or two months after five years of service. Laptops, badges and any other equipment are returned on the last working day. Unused vacation days are paid out at the final salary.

Access to company systems is removed at the end of the last working day. The People team schedules an exit conversation during the final week to collect feedback.
