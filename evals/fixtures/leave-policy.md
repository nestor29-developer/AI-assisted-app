# Leave Policy

This short policy explains leave at Northwind Traders.

## Vacation

Full-time employees accrue 1.5 vacation days per month. Unused vacation days expire on March 31 of the following year.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is paid for up to 10 days per calendar year. A doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive sick days.

## Family leave

Bereavement leave is 5 paid days for an immediate family member.
