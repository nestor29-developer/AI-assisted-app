# Northwind Employee Handbook

## Leave

Full-time employees accrue 1.5 vacation days per month, up to a maximum balance of 30 days.
Unused vacation days expire on March 31 of the following year.

## Remote work

Remote work requires written approval from the direct manager each quarter.
Employees working remotely must be reachable during core hours, nine to three local time.

## Expenses

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 60 dollars per day.
Receipts are required for any single expense above 25 dollars and must be submitted within 30 days.
